What is an earworm?

In simple terms, it's a tune that stays within us. In long, An earworm is a melody or a portion of a piece of music that is involuntarily repeated in one's mind. It is a tune that is stuck in one's head, especially as unwanted or repetitive.

I want to show that video game music is as powerful as art and other music by sharing small samples, whether you recognize the game or not.

Listen before reading. Start each song before reading its reason. Give yourself a chance to hear it and see if it gets stuck in your head. Then, read the reason why it is an earworm and see if you agree with it.

01

Super Mario Bros. - Ground Theme

"Ground Theme" was not just the music heard in Super Mario Bros.' first level or the Mario franchise's theme song; it was arguably the theme song for video games as a whole. Besides sounding like childlike wonder and being repetitive, Ground Theme was so iconic that it was used or referenced by almost everything related to gaming, whether it was connected to the '80s classic Super Mario Bros. or not.

02

Sonic the Hedgehog - Green Hill Zone

When Sonic was introduced to this song, Green Hill Zone became one of the biggest earworms in gaming history, so it somehow made it the unofficial and official theme for Sonic as a whole. It was always remixed when the classic Sonic level was honored, or remixed for jokes, memes, or stories you may have heard.

03

Final Fantasy - Victory Theme

The Final Fantasy "Victory Theme" has become one of the biggest earworms in gaming history because it is so synonymous with winning, with and without the original context, that even those who never played a Final Fantasy game recognized it. Part of the fun of keeping up with Final Fantasy was hearing each entry's spin on the fanfare.

04

Halo: Combat Evolved - Warthog Run

The original Halo: Combat Evolved featured the 8-minute "Truth and Reconciliation Suite." At its longest, the famous "Warthog Run" comprised only 3 minutes of the whole suite. The rousing earworm complemented the run so well that gamers often replayed the level just to relive the rush. The run was homaged across Halo games, complete with remixes.

05

Persona 5 - Last Surprise

The song was the perfect musical match to Persona 5's unique story and style, and one of the biggest earworms in gaming history. Whenever Persona 5's fight sequences were spoofed online or referenced, the jazzy "Last Surprise" accompanied them. Even outside the turn-based fights, it was almost impossible to forget, let alone ignore.

06

Undertale - Megalovania

From an EarthBound ROM hack to a Homestuck comic and the high-energy final boss theme in Undertale, Megalovania became a popular earworm. It appeared in games like Super Smash Bros. Ultimate and Taiko no Tatsujin, inspired remixes and covers, and reached mainstream media. In January 2024, Guinness World Records recognized it as the most-streamed video game soundtrack on Spotify.
